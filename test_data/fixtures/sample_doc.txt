Section 3: Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager
approval. Remote workers must be reachable during core hours, 10am to 4pm
in their local timezone, and must attend the weekly all-hands meeting on
Thursdays.

Section 7: Expense Reimbursement

Approved business expenses are reimbursed within 14 business days of
submission. Submissions must include an itemized receipt. Expenses over
$500 require director-level approval before submission.

Section 12: Data Handling

All customer data must be stored in approved systems only. Employees must
never export customer data to personal devices or unapproved cloud
storage. Any suspected data handling incident must be reported to the
security team within 24 hours.
